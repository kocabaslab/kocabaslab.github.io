## Principal Investigator

### Prof. Dr. Fatih Kocabaş
**Professor of Molecular Biology and Genetics**

<div class="row">
<div class="col-sm-9" markdown="1">

Prof. Dr. Fatih Kocabaş is a Professor of Molecular Biology and Genetics. He completed his B.S. at Middle East Technical University (ODTÜ) and earned his Ph.D. in Genetics and Development at the University of Texas Southwestern (UTSW) Medical Center at Dallas. He also holds a B.S. in Health Management from Anadolu University.

His scientific training includes collaborative research with Ron Vale and Susan Fisher at UCSF, and with Hesham Sadek, Alec Zhang, and Eric Olson at UTSW, alongside a research fellowship at the California Institute of Technology (Caltech) and stem cell training at Stanford University.

He has published more than seventy peer-reviewed articles with over 3,600 citations, featuring contributions in leading journals such as *Nature*, *Cell Stem Cell*, and *Blood*. He has directed more than fifteen funded projects spanning stem cell biology, regenerative medicine, cancer research, and gene editing, supported by TÜBİTAK, EIC, ERA-CVD, EU Marie Curie, ICGEB, THD, TÜBA, and the Ministry of Development’s Cell and Gene Therapies R&D Center initiative. He is the founder of Meinox, a biotechnology initiative developing targeted cancer therapeutics, and currently serves as the Editor-in-Chief of the *Turkish Journal of Biology*.
